Introduction

In science, rectify means to make flat. So RELU rectifies any line in its negative zone — by turning every negative number into zero!

🎉 Big moment! For the first time, we're building a multilayer model — with 2 LAYERS!

Here's what our 2-layer model looks like. Cool, right? 😎

But wait — there's more! We're also introducing two ACTS: ACT1 and ACT2.

What is an ACT?

Any ACT is simply a formula.

What does an ACT do?

In a NEURON, an ACT takes in a SUM and transforms it into the final number — which becomes that NEURON'S official RESULT. Magic! ✨

So every ACT is simply a formula that behaves in an interesting way and has its own superpower.

Today, we meet two famous ACTS named RELU and SIGI.

Here, RELU acts as ACT1 and SIGI acts as ACT2.

Let's dive in! 🚀

ACT1 (RELU)

Let's begin with our first figure, RELU.

RELU's superpower is known as "Perfect Gate".

Imagine RELU is a magician. Its unique ability is a game called "SAVE or ZERO", which it plays with every SUM1 that passes through it.

Every time a SUM1 enters RELU, the magician asks one simple question: "Are you positive or negative?"

  • SAVE (Positives): If SUM1 is positive (like 0.5 or 3.0), RELU SAVES it. SUM1 passes through completely unchanged. So, 0.5 stays 0.5, and 3.0 stays 3.0.
  • ZERO (Negatives): If SUM1 is negative (like −0.5 or −3.0), RELU ZEROS it, turning SUM1 into 0.0. Only 0.0 passes through. So, −0.5 becomes 0.0, and −3.0 becomes 0.0.

In other words, RELU takes in a SUM1 and sends out the maximum of that SUM1 and 0.0.

The RELU formula:

RELU = max(SUM1, 0.0)

INFLUENCE (RELU)

When ACT1 is RELU, calculating the INFLUENCE of SUM1 on ACT1 is incredibly simple:

  • When SUM1 is positive: ACT1 = SUM1. Therefore, influence_SUM1_ACT1 is 1.0.
  • When SUM1 is negative: ACT1 = 0.0. Therefore, influence_SUM1_ACT1 is 0.0.

Therefore, when ACT1 is RELU:

influence_SUM1_ACT1 = 1.0 (if SUM1 > 0.0)

influence_SUM1_ACT1 = 0.0 (if SUM1 < 0.0)

influence_SUM1_ACT1 = 0.0 (if SUM1 = 0.0)

Very easy!

ACT2 (SIGI)

Let's look at our second figure, SIGI.

SIGI's superpower is called "Gentle Squeeze".

Imagine SIGI as another magician. Its special ability is a game called "SQUEEZE IT BETWEEN ZERO AND ONE", which it plays with every SUM2 that passes through it.

Every time a SUM2 arrives, SIGI doesn't ask questions. Instead, it squeezes that SUM2 — no matter how big or small — into a tiny living room between 0.0 and 1.0.

  • Large positive numbers (like 3.0 or 5.0) get squeezed very close to 1.0 — but never quite reach it.
  • Large negative numbers (like −3.0 or −5.0) get squeezed very close to 0.0 — but never quite reach it.
  • Zero (0.0) lands right in the middle — it becomes 0.5.

In other words, SIGI takes in any SUM2 and sends out a number that is always between 0.0 and 1.0.

The SIGI formula:

SIGI = 1 / (1 + 1/2.71828SUM2)

Don't worry about this fancy formula — just know it's a gentle squeeze!

SIGI gets its name from its curve, which looks like the letter "S".

So SIGI is gentle, smooth, and always keeps numbers between 0.0 and 1.0. It's like a calm teacher who never raises their voice too high or drops too low.

INFLUENCE (SIGI)

When ACT2 is SIGI, calculating the INFLUENCE of SUM2 on ACT2 is also incredibly simple:

influence_SUM2_ACT2 = ACT2 * (1 - ACT2)

Very easy!

Some very clever mathematicians figured out this super simple formula above. 👍


Therefore, when ACT2 is SIGI:

  • If SUM2 is near 0.0: influence_SUM2_ACT2 is at its highest — around 0.25. That means a small change in SUM2 makes a noticeable change in ACT2.
  • If SUM2 is very positive or very negative: influence_SUM2_ACT2 gets closer to 0.0. That means changing SUM2 barely affects ACT2 — it's already squeezed to its limit!

🧠 BRAIN

So, we're going to build a machine brain that can recommend whether you should go to a party or not.

In this scenario, each NEURON of LAYER1 receives three INPUTS at once.

These three INPUTS represent the three pieces of knowledge, and the OUTPUT represents the recommended action: GO or STAY.

If the OUTPUT is 0.5 or greater, it means GO. Otherwise, if the OUTPUT is less than 0.5, it means STAY.

Therefore, during inference, we'll round the OUTPUT — mapping 1 to GO and 0 to STAY.

Let's call this brain the Party-Recommender.

As previously noted, it will have multiple PARAMETERS: WEIGHTS and BIASES.

The brain will be just a formula.

Change Rule

The brain changes each PARAMETER using the FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE:

FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE for PARAMETER
PARAMETER_change = ERROR * influence_PARAMETER_OUTPUT * PACE
PARAMETER = PARAMETER + PARAMETER_change

Because the brain has multiple PARAMETERS — WEIGHTS and BIASES — we obtain these two CHANGE RULES:

FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE for WEIGHT
WEIGHT_change = ERROR * influence_WEIGHT_OUTPUT * PACE
WEIGHT = WEIGHT + WEIGHT_change
FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE for BIAS
BIAS_change = ERROR * influence_BIAS_OUTPUT * PACE
BIAS = BIAS + BIAS_change

Don't worry — each step is simple. Just follow the calculations below.

EXAMPLE 1:

Backward Pass — Backpropagation — CONCEPTION

In this LESSON, all PARAMETERS compete for the largest CHANGES. The PARAMETERS with the largest ERROR and INFLUENCE — calculated as ERROR * influence_PARAMETER_OUTPUT — receive the largest CHANGES because changing them has the greatest effect on changing the OUTPUT and reducing the ERROR.

Because PACE affects all PARAMETERS by the exact same multiplier, it does not affect their competition.

The core logic is simple:

high ERROR * high INFLUENCE = larger CHANGE

All PARAMETER CHANGES are calculated exactly this way.
