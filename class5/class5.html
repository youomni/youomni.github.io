Now, about ACTS.

There are several ACTS in AI.

What is an ACT?

An ACT is simply a formula.

What does an ACT do?

An ACT takes in SUM and transforms it into a final number — which becomes the NEURON'S official RESULT. Magic! ✨

Here, the ACT is called SQZE (pronounced Squeeze) — a formula that acts in an interesting way and has its own superpower.

Let's dive in! 🚀

ACT (SQZE)

Let’s take a closer look at this ACT — called SQZE.

SQZE has a cool superpower called the "Gentle Squeeze".

Imagine SQZE as a friendly magician. 🪄 It loves a game called "ZERO TO ONE", which it plays with every SUM that passes through it.

Every time a SUM arrives, SQZE gently squeezes it into a cozy space between 0.0 and 1.0.

  • Large positive numbers (like 3.0 or 5.0) get squeezed very close to 1.0 — but never quite reach it.
  • Large negative numbers (like −3.0 or −5.0) get squeezed very close to 0.0 — but never quite reach it.
  • Zero (0.0) lands right in the middle — it becomes 0.5.

In other words, SQZE takes in any SUM and sends out a number that is always between 0.0 and 1.0.

The SQZE formula:

SQZE = 1 / (1 + 1/2.71828SUM)

Don't worry about this fancy formula — just know it's a gentle squeeze!

Plus, SQZE's curve looks like its first letter — "S".

So SQZE is gentle, smooth, and always keeps numbers between 0.0 and 1.0. It's like a calm teacher who never raises their voice too high or drops too low.

INFLUENCE (SQZE)

When ACT is SQZE, calculating the INFLUENCE of SUM on ACT is incredibly simple:

influence_SUM_ACT = ACT * (1 - ACT)

Very easy!

Some very clever mathematicians figured out this super simple formula above. 👍


Therefore, when ACT is SQZE:

  • If SUM is near 0.0: influence_SUM_ACT is at its highest — around 0.25. That means a small change in SUM makes a noticeable change in ACT.
  • If SUM is very positive or very negative: influence_SUM_ACT gets closer to 0.0. That means changing SUM barely affects ACT — as the ACT is already squeezed to its limit!

🧠 BRAIN

So, we're going to build a machine brain that can recommend whether you should go to a party or not.

In this scenario, each NEURON of LAYER1 receives three INPUTS at once.

These three INPUTS represent the three pieces of knowledge, and the OUTPUT represents the recommended action: GO or STAY.

If the OUTPUT is 0.5 or greater, it means GO. Otherwise, if the OUTPUT is less than 0.5, it means STAY.

Therefore, during inference, we'll round the OUTPUT — mapping 1 to GO and 0 to STAY.

Let's call this brain the Party-Recommender.

As previously noted, it will have multiple PARAMETERS: WEIGHTS and BIASES.

Change Rule

The brain changes each PARAMETER using the FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE:

FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE for PARAMETER
PARAMETER_change = ERROR * influence_PARAMETER_OUTPUT * PACE
PARAMETER = PARAMETER + PARAMETER_change

Because the brain has multiple PARAMETERS — WEIGHTS and BIASES — we obtain these two CHANGE RULES:

FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE for WEIGHT
WEIGHT_change = ERROR * influence_WEIGHT_OUTPUT * PACE
WEIGHT = WEIGHT + WEIGHT_change
FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE for BIAS
BIAS_change = ERROR * influence_BIAS_OUTPUT * PACE
BIAS = BIAS + BIAS_change

Don't worry — each step is simple. Just follow the calculations below.

Backward Pass — Backpropagation — CONCEPTION

In this LESSON, all PARAMETERS compete for the largest CHANGES. The PARAMETERS with the largest ERROR and INFLUENCE — calculated as ERROR * influence_PARAMETER_OUTPUT — receive the largest CHANGES because changing them has the greatest effect on changing the OUTPUT and reducing the ERROR.

Because PACE affects all PARAMETERS by the exact same multiplier, it does not affect their competition.

The core logic is simple:

high ERROR * high INFLUENCE = larger CHANGE

All PARAMETER CHANGES are calculated exactly this way.
